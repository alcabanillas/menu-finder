## Why

Roadmap item **MF-22.5** (`context/roadmap.md`). Since MF-22.4, `/planner` searches and shows the top 5, but the user cannot choose any of them: the only way to choose is still the provisional random button of MF-43. Choosing also needs a decision the system took alone until now: the week. Today the server puts a new menu on this week's Monday when it is free and on next Monday otherwise, so a user cannot prepare next week while this week is still empty, nor fix this week's menu once it is set. The definitive Buscar mock lets the user choose the week (decided by the author on 2026-10-16). This change closes the planner's loop (UI-flujo-semanal, UI-planner-buscador): the user chooses one of the five menus for this week or next week and goes on to see it in `/menu`.

## What Changes

- **The week is the user's choice.** `menu-selection` stops computing the week from the existing selections: the request says `current` or `next`, and the server turns it into this week's or next week's Monday from today in Europe/Madrid. A date is still never read from the request.
- **This week is locked once next week is chosen.** When next week already has a menu, this week's menu cannot be changed, because the current shopping list would change under the user (decided by the author on 2026-10-16). The server refuses it, and the selector shows "Esta semana" disabled.
- **The week selector.** "Eligiendo menú para" sits above the search box, after the design system's `PlannerScreen.jsx` (`WeekTarget`): two options, "Esta semana" and "La que viene", each with its dates ("5 – 11 oct") and its menu or "Sin menú". It starts on the first week that can take a menu and is empty, and otherwise on next week. Under it, a line says where the menu will go ("El menú que elijas irá a la semana del 12 oct.") or that it will replace the one there. It replaces the summary of this week and next week of MF-43.
- **Choosing from a card.** Each card says which week its menu goes to: "Elegir para el 12 oct" when that week is empty, and "Cambiar el menú del 5 oct" (secondary) when it has a menu; the card of the menu that week already has reads "Menú del 5 oct", disabled, with the olive double border. A server action checks the session, reads the menu number and the week, and calls a new use case, `chooseMenu`, which delegates to the `selectMenu` service; `selectMenu` stays a service because the random button still calls it (ADR-001: a use case never calls another). After choosing, the results stay and the selector says "Menú 22 para la semana del 12 oct." with a "Ver semana" link to `/menu` on that week.
- **Design system version 1792255497-a1ff** (decided by the author on 2026-10-17): no greeting and a heading for screen readers only; the locked week and the "Si eliges otro, lo sustituye" help inside the option cards; "¿Qué te apetece?"; "Elegir uno al azar", secondary, beside the search box (under it on a phone), chooses for the selected week; a confirmation box, in the selector for the random button and in the card for a menu, before a menu replaces the one a week has. "Ir a Buscar" of an empty week in Menú opens `/planner?week=current|next`. The random button leaves its own form: it is one more submit of the choose form, with `random`.
- One Sentry breadcrumb in the browser when a menu is chosen and one when the answer arrives, with the outcome and the week only.
- Not in this change: the polarity tokens (MF-22.8); "Ver semana" on the cards (the definitive mock moved it under the selector). No warning about lost ticks when a menu is replaced (decided by the author on 2026-10-17). The confirmation before replacing, the random button for the selected week and the sticky search box, planned for MF-22.8 and MF-22.9, are here because the design system brought them in.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `menu-selection`: the start date comes from the week the user chooses, `current` or `next`, not from the existing selections; a new requirement validates the week; a new one locks this week once next week has a menu. The examples of the replacement and ownership requirements name the week.
- `menu-planner`: the summary of the weeks is replaced by the week selector; new requirements for choosing a menu from the ranking for that week, the chosen card, the messages, and the session and input check of the action; the cards gain the choose button; the random button chooses for the selector's starting week.
- `error-reporting`: the browser breadcrumbs of choosing a menu, with the outcome and the week only.

## Impact

- **Code:** `src/domain/selection/week.ts` (the Monday of a chosen week, the lock and the starting week, in place of `startsOnFor`), `src/application/services/selection/select-menu.ts` (a `week` input), `src/application/use-cases/menu/` (a new `choose-menu.ts`; `select-random-menu.ts` uses the starting week; `current-selections.ts` gives way to a use case for the two weeks of the selector), `src/application/dto/selection-summary.ts`, `src/composition/web/menu-use-cases.ts`, `src/app/(signed-in)/planner/` (the page and a `chooseMenuAction`), `src/features/menu-planner/` (the selector, the choose state in `PlannerSearch`, the card's button; `SelectionSummary` removed), the week range format, which moves from `features/weekly-menu/` to `shared/` because a second feature uses it (ADR-001, Scope Rule), and `context/decisiones.md` ARQ-modelo-datos, whose selection rule changes.
- **Data touched:** the user's selections (one row per user and Monday). Replacing a week's selection deletes its shopping ticks (`ON DELETE CASCADE`, `postgres/migrations/006-user-shopping-item.sql`); the lock keeps this week's list safe once next week is chosen, and the confirmation asks before the rest. No request text or term reaches the action or Sentry.
- **Abuse:** a server action is a public POST endpoint. Without a session it redirects to `/login` before reading the form. The user comes from the session; only `menu` and `week` are read, and every other field, a date among them, is ignored. The menu is parsed as a positive integer and the week against `current` and `next`; anything else is refused and nothing is stored. The lock is checked on the server, so a forged `current` cannot bypass the disabled option. Choosing a menu outside the top 5 is allowed: it touches only the user's own selections. Repeated posts rewrite at most two rows per user and never call the model. A cross-site POST is stopped by Next.js, which compares `Origin` with `Host` for server actions, and by the `SameSite=Lax` session cookie.
- **OWASP:** A01 Broken Access Control (the session check, the user only from the session), A03 Injection (number and week validated before the parameterised query), A04 Insecure Design (the lock enforced on the server, not only in the UI; no unbounded writes), A09 (breadcrumbs without request data).
- **Decisions relied on:** UI-flujo-semanal, UI-planner-buscador, UI-design-system, ARQ-hexagonal (ADR-001, "Casos de uso y services" and the Scope Rule), OPS-observabilidad, SEG-datos-nutricionista. **Changes ARQ-modelo-datos**, which says the start date is computed from the existing selections ("al elegir sin menú activo esta semana empieza este lunes; con menú activo, el lunes siguiente"): now the user chooses the week, and this week is locked once next week is chosen (decided by the author on 2026-10-16). Deviations from the mock (version `1792178100-1406`):
  - The mock had no locked state for "Esta semana"; version 1792255497-a1ff has it, and this change follows it.
  - The mock's "Prueba con" suggestions stay out (MF-22.4).
